Selection and formatting help

Click to select, Ctrl/Command-click to toggle, Shift-click to select a range. Drag to select text; selected text is the styling target even when blocks are selected. Text-align and Headings apply to blocks; clear text selection first. Hover their menu entries to choose values, or use click/Right Arrow. Enter/Space supports the same modifiers. Right-click or Ctrl-right-click for Selection actions, including the Excerpts submenu; Context Menu or Shift+F10 also opens it. Colored lines on the right identify saved excerpt membership; click a line to select its excerpt, or right-click it to edit the name, save or delete directly in the menu. Selections use complete original source blocks in source order, not corrected wording.
