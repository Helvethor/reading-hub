Catalogue book membership

Membership belongs to the catalogue work {{ book.title|default:version.title }}, not this edition or an editor draft. It controls whether source URLs are separate works or resolve to this book.

Titles, roles, reading order and included content are managed in the document editor's section navigation. Attaching does not insert content; detaching does not remove any section from drafts or publications. No parent or donor book is rebuilt.

This creates a source-generated edition from the currently attached URLs. Listed members follow the retained contents-page order; members without a retained ordering are appended with a warning, and listed non-members are excluded with a warning. It does not change membership, drafts or saved publications. An explicitly selected reader version remains selected.
